# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management knowledge hub. This folder contains standardized guidance for running iterative, customer-focused projects.

## Quick Start

New to OctoAcme? Start with our [Project Management Overview](octoacme-project-management-overview.md) to understand core principles, roles, and artifacts.

## About OctoAcme Project Management

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative approach to project management designed to deliver value consistently while maintaining team psychological safety and data-driven decision-making. Our methodology emphasizes:

- **Customer-first mindset**: Prioritize customer value and usability in every decision
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments rather than large monolithic releases
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named Project Manager (PM) and Product Manager (PdM) responsible for coordinating delivery and defining outcomes
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact against success metrics and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback, learning, and continuous improvement across the team

The OctoAcme framework encompasses six key lifecycle phases: **Initiation**, **Planning**, **Execution & Tracking**, **Risk Management & Communication**, **Release & Deployment**, and **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement**. Combined with clearly defined roles (Developers, Product Managers, Project Managers, and QA/Testing), this creates a repeatable, scalable approach to project delivery.

## Project Lifecycle Guides

### 1. **Initiation** — Define and Validate

[Project Initiation Guide](octoacme-project-initiation.md) — Confirm business need, identify stakeholders, and make a go/no-go decision.

**When to use**: Whenever a new project idea or feature proposal is ready to be explored.

**Key deliverables**: Project One-pager, stakeholder list, high-level timeline, initial risk list, and resource needs.

### 2. **Planning** — Create Your Roadmap

[Project Planning](octoacme-project-planning.md) — Break work into increments, estimate scope, and define milestones.

**When to use**: After project initiation is approved and you're ready to create an actionable plan.

**Key activities**: Kickoff meeting, prioritized backlog creation, scope estimation, Definition of Done, dependency identification, and release plan.

### 3. **Execution & Tracking** — Build & Deliver

[Execution & Tracking](octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) — Manage day-to-day work, quality, and progress toward milestones.

**When to use**: During active development sprints and iterations.

**Key practices**: Daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, PR-based workflows, automated testing, velocity tracking, and blocker escalation.

### 4. **Risk & Communication** — Mitigate & Align

[Risk Management & Communication](octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) — Identify risks, manage dependencies, and keep stakeholders informed.

**When to use**: Throughout the project lifecycle, especially during planning and execution.

**Key responsibilities**: Maintain risk register, assess impact and likelihood, develop mitigation plans, and provide regular stakeholder updates.

### 5. **Release & Deployment** — Go Live Safely

[Release & Deployment Guide](octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) — Standardize how we release features and manage rollback.

**When to use**: When preparing to move code to production.

**Key requirements**: Pre-release checklist, smoke testing, deployment verification, rollback planning, and release notes.

### 6. **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement** — Learn & Improve

[Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) — Capture learnings and convert them into actionable improvements.

**When to use**: After each sprint, release, or important milestone; also after incidents.

**Key activities**: Facilitate team retrospectives, track action items, measure impact of improvements, and celebrate wins.

## Core Roles at a Glance

- **Project Manager (PM)**: Coordinates delivery, manages schedules, risks, and communications
- **Product Manager (PdM)**: Defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success
- **Developers**: Implement features, collaborate on design, and ensure testability
- **QA/Testing**: Validate quality and acceptance criteria
- **Stakeholders**: Provide inputs, approvals, and strategic guidance

## Key Artifacts

Every OctoAcme project produces and maintains:

- **Project Charter / One-pager** — High-level business case and goals
- **Roadmap and Release Plan** — Milestones and delivery timeline
- **Sprint/Iteration Backlog** — Prioritized work with acceptance criteria
- **Risk Register** — Identified risks with impact, likelihood, and mitigation plans
- **Definition of Done** — Clear quality and completion standards
- **Retrospective notes** — Learnings and action items

## Communication Cadence

- **Daily**: Team standups (15 min) — focus on progress, blockers, dependencies
- **Weekly**: PM + PdM sync, delivery team updates, risk reviews
- **Monthly**: Stakeholder updates and progress reviews
- **As needed**: Escalations, incident communication, and ad-hoc alignment
